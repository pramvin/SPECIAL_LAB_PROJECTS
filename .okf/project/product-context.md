- **Problem (hypothesis, no user research yet):** People find it hard to gather stock data and news themselves, don't know which news to trust, and don't know what to do next.
- **Solution:** Idea cards per risk profile: reasons from real numbers + news + stop/target (ATR) + position size + an AI reading of the news (badge "AI · Not validated", does not affect the score).
- **Owner's concept:** The filter answers "is the news real / is the source credible?" · the LLM answers "good or bad for the stock, and why?" · news is credible when an identifiable party says it themselves.
- **Flow:** Login → 5-question onboarding → Trade ideas (`?h=1w|1m|3m`) → cards + Top news · Watchlist and stock search are separate menus.
- **Trust principle:** Separate facts / user data / examples / AI output · no data = "unknown", never a fake number.
